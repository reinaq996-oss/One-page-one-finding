“What predicts a final grade, and which of those would you refuse to use?”

Grades can signal performance.
They should never define potential.

How Strongly Is Each Factor Associated with the Final Grade?

Previous grades dominate. Background factors are far weaker—and students do not choose them.

Average Final Grade by Weekly Study Time

Grades rise, then dip slightly in the smallest group. An association only.

Average Final Grade by Parents’ Education

Grades rise with parents’ education, but this describes a student’s background, not effort or ability.

Source: Cortez, P. (2008). Student Performance. UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: 10.24432/C5TG7T. Portuguese-language dataset; 649 students from two Portuguese secondary schools.  ·  Descriptive associations, not causal effects. Findings may not generalize beyond this dataset.
